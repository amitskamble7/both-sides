Design preview by Both Sides, not the live website. Only the design changes. Your WordPress, editor, stories, web addresses, Rank Math and AdSense stay as they are; the option you choose becomes a new WordPress theme.
Advert spaces. Every space has a fixed size, so the page never jumps when an advert arrives. When Google has an advert, it covers the space; when Google has none (AdSense marks the space "unfilled"), your own banner underneath shows instead of an empty box. Switch "Google advert spaces" above to see the sizes. Pictures.
