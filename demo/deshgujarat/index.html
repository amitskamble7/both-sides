DeshGujarat: new design, four options

Option 4 · The Daily Edition, with your changes

Option 2, the calm newspaper-style design you chose, reworked from your notes: on phones one continuous feed with your top stories first, mostly headline with a small picture, a big picture where your team wants one, categories always at hand, your Shorts, search and a morning email. On a computer it keeps the broad front page.

Design preview by Both Sides, not the live website. Only the design changes. Your WordPress, editor, stories, web addresses, Rank Math and AdSense stay as they are; the option you choose becomes your new WordPress theme.

What changed, from your notes

  1. One continuous feed on phones. Your top stories first, then every story, newest first. More stories load by themselves as the reader scrolls; there is no last page.
  2. Mostly headline with a small picture (X style), so a reader sees five or six stories on one screen. Any story can get the big picture (YouTube style) when your team wants it to stand out.
  3. Top stories in the order your team sets, and the Featured story stays at the top for as long as you choose.
  4. Categories always at hand. On phones the category strip stays at the top of the screen while the reader scrolls, above every advert.
  5. No Gujarat or Ahmedabad blocks on the home page, since most of your stories are in both. They stay in the category strip.
  6. Shorts row from both your YouTube channels, filled by itself, and your newest long videos further down.
  7. Search on every page: a box on computers, the magnifier on phones. Try it in the preview.
  8. "Get DeshGujarat every morning": an email sign-up made from your own feed, your WhatsApp channel and phone alerts.
  9. Soft off-white background, the same paper tone as the app idea you liked, easier on the eyes than pure white.
Left out on purpose: a row of X posts. Live X posts need X's own embed code, which is heavy and would slow your pages again. Your X account stays one tap away in your own banners and the footer. Adverts: on phones, Google's Auto ads now put a tall advert bar at the top of your pages. In your AdSense Auto ads settings the anchor advert can be kept to the bottom of the screen; then nothing covers the categories.
