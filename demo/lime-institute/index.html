Your export journey

From Rajkot to your first buyer, in seven steps.

  1. 1Choose your product
  2. 2Get your IEC
  3. 3Find your buyer
  4. 4Price it right
  5. 5Prepare the documents
  6. 6Ship and clear customs
  7. 7Get paid, claim benefits
Step 1

Choose your product

  • Which Indian products are growing abroad, and where
  • Gujarat's strengths: ceramics, engineering parts, brass, cotton, groundnuts
  • Market research before you spend a rupee
Free tool: Which product should I export?
Step 2

Get your IEC

  • The Import Export Code, step by step on the DGFT site
  • Your firm, GST and bank details in order
  • RCMC registration with the right export council
Free tool: IEC checklist
Step 3

Find your buyer

  • Buyer lists, trade portals and international fairs
  • Checking a buyer before you ship
  • Writing to buyers: email formats that get answers
Free tool: Trade fairs this month
Step 4

Price it right

  • Costing: from factory price to FOB and CIF
  • Incoterms in plain words, and who pays for what
  • Duty, freight and insurance in your quote
Free tool: Export price calculator (below)
Step 5

Prepare the documents

  • Invoice, packing list, shipping bill, bill of lading
  • Certificates of origin and the right licences
  • 55+ export documents, practised on real cases
Free tool: Document checklist
Step 6

Ship and clear customs

  • Choosing a CHA and a freight forwarder
  • Container sizes and how much fits
  • What happens at the port; a visit with your batch
Free tool: Container load calculator
Step 7

Get paid, claim benefits

  • Letters of credit, advance and open-account terms
  • Bank and RBI rules for export money
  • Government schemes and incentives you can claim
Free tool: Today's exchange rates
