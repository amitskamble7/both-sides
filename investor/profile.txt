OUR EDGE: THE AI EDITORIAL NEWSROOM HARNESS
==========================================================================

We have built the system that turns incoming news coverage into a finished
Both Sides card. The harness coordinates specialized AI work with deterministic
code, source checks and publishing controls.

This combination lets us automate repetitive production work while keeping
clear rules around what moves through the pipeline. Each part supports the
same objective: produce a readable account of the event and its competing
positions through an efficient, repeatable workflow.

The harness performs nine connected activities:

1. COLLECT COVERAGE
Monitor 76 Indian outlets and gather additional coverage through the major
news search indexes and our own recent-story index.

2. ORGANIZE TOPICS
Use our own code to group related headlines and identify coverage of the
same event.

3. SELECT STORIES
Apply timing, freshness and duplication rules to determine which topics
advance through the publishing workflow.

4. FILTER SOURCE MATERIAL
Use relevance filtering to identify articles about the selected story and
reduce unrelated material before it reaches the AI stages.

5. DRAFT THE CARD
Coordinate AI writing of the event account, the central claims and the
explanation of each position.

6. CHECK AGAINST SOURCES
Run an AI checking stage against the collected articles, followed by code
that checks supporting quotations against source text.

7. CONTROL STORY VERSIONS
Associate checks with the corresponding story version so publishing uses
the text that passed through the checking workflow.

8. PREPARE THE IMAGE
Gather candidate photographs, select a suitable image, crop it to the card
format and preserve its credit. Labeled illustrations provide a fallback.

9. PUBLISH AND MONITOR
Deliver the completed card to the apps through Firebase, with publishing
gates, status monitoring, alerts and recovery procedures.

Our principal AI stages cover writing, checking and picture selection. Code
handles collection, organization, filtering, workflow state and many of the
checks surrounding those AI stages. This design concentrates model use on
the work that calls for it.

THE READER EXPERIENCE
==========================================================================

Every Both Sides card brings together:

WHAT HAPPENED
A concise account of the event gives the reader a starting point.

THE COMPETING CLAIMS
Each side's central position appears in a short, accessible form.

THE ARGUMENTS BEHIND THEM
The reader can explore how the sides explain their positions and reach
their own conclusion.

This format makes comparison part of the reading experience. It reduces
the effort of moving between separate creators, articles and editorial
voices to understand the disagreement.

The product is free. AI-written content is identified as such. Our publishing
workflow checks supporting quotations against source text, while the reader
retains the freedom to form a judgment.

SLIDE 01: BOTH SIDES

One story. Both sides. Your judgment.

Both Sides is a news app for India. Social media shows each person one side.

SLIDE 02: THE APP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every story is one card. Both sides on it.

- At the top, what happened, in one line nobody disputes.
- Then each side's claim, in a sentence, side by side.
- Then each side's full case, in its own words. We never pick a winner.

Free, no ads, no trackers. Written by AI, and says so. Fact-checked before it

SLIDE 03: THE MARKET TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

News comes from two kinds of places: news apps, built for speed, and
traditional outlets, built around their own reporting. Both kinds tell each
story in one voice.

SLIDE 04: WHERE INDIA READS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

News already lives inside the apps people open every day.

Among India's online news users, over half use YouTube for news each week

SLIDE 05: TWO JOBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

News has two jobs. Both Sides does the second one.

Someone finds what happened: reporters, and agencies like ANI. Then someone

SLIDE 06: A HABIT PER GENERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The format changes. The need does not.

Our grandparents read the paper every morning. Our parents watched the nine
o'clock bulletin. Younger people follow creators on YouTube and Instagram,

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 07: THE BUBBLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Social feeds split people into bubbles.

You follow the creators you like and avoid the ones you do not, and the feed
learns from every click. Soon each side feels it is seeing the whole world
when it sees only one part.

Both Sides puts both bubbles in one place. No creator to like or dislike.
Just both arguments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

One story. Two strong cases. You decide.

We start with what happened, make the strongest case for each side, and
stop. About ten minutes a day, and no verdict from us. The reader gets
enough to understand the disagreement; the judgment stays theirs.

Gen Z and millennials who will not be told how to feel.

They see politics and public issues in their feeds all day and do not want
to join a team. Not the left, not the right: the curious middle, which we
believe is the biggest group. Their request is simple: do not spam me, do
not tell me how to feel, show me both cases and I will decide. That is our
starting belief. Retention will prove it.

SLIDE 11: THE NEWSROOM TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

How other news outlets and news apps work today.

What goes in, what comes out.

Inside: nine stations, three AI rounds per story, eight publish gates, eight

SLIDE 16: WHAT IT TOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The reader sees one card. It stands on nine layers of code and checks:

- Three AI rounds per story, under strict contracts: a reply is accepted
  whole or rejected whole.
- Nothing the AI wrote reaches a card unverified: every line is checked
  against its sources.
- Eight publish gates, each one a hard stop: fail one and the story is
  parked, never pushed through.
- An on-topic filter of our own, with no AI, tuned on 6,300 real headlines:
  it drops 85% of the pile and keeps 97% of the right articles.
- 76 outlets watched round the clock, plus three global indexes; up to 800
  articles gathered for every story.
- An engine that never forces a story through: a story is published right,
  or not at all.
- It checks itself round the clock: eight health checks, five healing
  sweeps, verified backups.
- Locked down and tested end to end: 53 API routes, 14 screens, 125
  configuration keys, six test suites.
- A 5,662-line manual, just to describe what is there.

Everything under the water exists so the tip is right, every time, with
nobody watching. The full drawings are shown on the first call.
