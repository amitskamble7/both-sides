01The desk, part by part

Six parts, from the first source to the published post.

Each part takes one routine job off your team. Your approval rules decide where a person steps in.

AutomatedYour rules
  1. 01Automated

    Gather

    Watches the sources you choose, all day: websites, feeds, posts on X, press releases and your own documents. Groups them into stories.

  2. 02Automated

    Rank

    Scores every story for your readers, learned from what you already publish, with a short brief on why it matters.

  3. 03Automated

    Write

    Drafts the article from the gathered reports, in your voice, your format and your language.

  4. 04Automated

    Fact check

    An independent check of every line against the sources. What they do not support is corrected, or held with the reason.

  5. 05Automated

    Translate

    Eight Indian languages and English. Each translation gets its own check.

  6. 06Your rules

    Approve and publish

    An editor’s yes on each piece, or straight to your website and handles. You set the rule.

Also in the desk

Pictures
The source’s own released picture, a licensed and credited one, or one drawn for the story. Every picture is described.
Search details
An SEO title, a description and tags for every article.
Sections
One section for each brand, city or region, each with its own sources and language.
Duplicate guard
Flags a story you have already published, or one that is already with an editor.
Media reading
Reads a picture or video post and reports what it says.
Daily report
What went out, what was held and why, on your phone every evening. An alert only when something needs a person.

02Where it publishes

Your website, your handles, your app.

The desk publishes the way your team does today, each piece in the format of the place it goes.

Website

Articles on your site

Published as a staff writer would: your design, your categories, your author line, with a picture and its description. WordPress and other site systems.

Social media

Posts on every handle

The moment an article goes up, the desk posts it on every handle of that brand: a picture card in your own post design and a caption written for each platform.

App

An app, if you want one

No app yet? We build one for Android and iPhone, published on Google Play and the App Store and fed by the same desk.

Your team

One screen for your editors

Anything that needs a person arrives on one screen with its sources and its fact check, to approve or reject.

04Safeguards

Safe to leave running.

The desk is built to hold back rather than guess.

Checked before it goes out

Every line is matched against its sources before anything is published.

Held, with the reason

A line the sources do not support is corrected or held for a person, with the reason. It never goes out as written.

Credited pictures

Every picture carries its credit, or is drawn for the story and described.

A cap on every bill

The AI service and X each take a monthly cap, so a bill never runs past it.

Your data stays yours

Your accounts, your server and your archive.

Your AI policy

Whether and how AI is labelled on your pieces follows your own policy.
